## Brand & Style

This design system translates the distinguished institutional heritage of the ballet academy into an agile, highly refined mobile administrative interface. Tailored expressly for handheld operation (optimized for 390px viewports), it fuses classical theatrical dignity with modern operational efficiency. The aesthetic departs from cold, generic enterprise software, instilling an atmosphere of disciplined artistry, poise, and architectural order.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with warm **Minimalism**. Clean, light surfaces reminiscent of stage parchment and ivory dance studios host crisp, dark navy structural elements, accentuated by classical heritage burgundy and subtle amber gold. Every interaction conveys calm authority, structural legibility, and premium craftsmanship, empowering directors, instructors, and academy administrators with frictionless mobile workflows.

## Colors

The mobile palette relies on warm, light foundational surfaces anchored by deep navy and academy insignia tones:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** Deep Navy. Forms top app bars, primary touch targets, and structural emphasis. It anchors the workspace with authoritative gravitas.
- **Secondary (`#8B2635`):** Refined Burgundy, drawn directly from the academy's heritage insignia. Applied to crucial badges, primary alert calls, and ceremonial actions.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059`):** Ballet Gold. Used for high-prestige indicators, active states, certification ratings, and refined trim.
- **Surfaces (`#FCF9F8`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F4F3F1`):** Warm Ivory creates an eye-resting canvas that prevents glare during prolonged usage, while pure white lifts foreground interactive cards.
- **Neutrals & Borders (`#1A1A1A`, `#5A5A5A`, `#E8E5E1`):** Calibrated for high-contrast legibility in varying lighting conditions, with soft grey borders delineating information without visual noise.
- **Functional Semantics:** Standardized emerald (`#1E7E4E`), warning amber (`#D97706`), and alert red (`#DC2626`) pair with 10% soft tinted backgrounds for quick status appraisal on small screens.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the theatrical cadence of **Oswald** with the clean, utilitarian legibility of **Work Sans**:

- **Display & Headings (Oswald):** Used in uppercase or title casing for section headers, modal banners, studio labels, and key statistics. Condensed horizontal proportions provide high informational density without line-wrapping on narrow mobile screens.
- **Body & Controls (Work Sans):** Neutral and legible, providing effortless scannability for rosters, academic schedules, student dossiers, and administrative notes.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-caps` in Oswald uppercase with 0.08em tracking gives section headers, table headers, and metadata tags a crisp architectural finish.
- **Mobile Scale Adjustments:** Display sizes cap at 32px to ensure titles remain compact, preventing unwanted vertical inflation in dense task-oriented workflows.

## Layout & Spacing

The mobile layout model is built on an ergonomic 4px/8px modular scale, optimized for one-handed thumb interaction within a standard 390px mobile viewport:

- **Grid Architecture:** A fluid single-column mobile layout framed by standard 16px screen margins. Internal cards and multi-column tiles utilize 12px gutters.
- **Thumb-Zone Optimization:** Critical navigation and trigger points are anchored within the lower 40% of the screen. Top app bars remain restricted to branding, identity context, and filter sheet triggers.
- **Navigation Chrome:** A persistent bottom navigation bar (64px height + safe area inset) keeps primary academy modules (Roster, Classes, Financials, Profile) immediately accessible.
- **Vertical Stacking:** Content sections maintain 20px–24px gaps, while intra-card groups space at 8px–12px intervals, achieving clear grouped hierarchy without dense clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through calm tonal layering and featherweight ambient shadows rather than stark elevation planes:

- **Base Layer:** Ivory backdrop (`#FCF9F8`) hosts cards and surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Panels):** Foreground containers sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px border (`#E8E5E1`). Depth is reinforced with a warm, subtle shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Action Sheets:** Raised action sheets and drawers use `0px -4px 24px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)` paired with a soft backdrop scrim (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.4)`).
- **Sticky Bars:** The bottom navigation bar and sticky summary footers feature a 1px top border (`#E8E5E1`) backed by 95% opacity white with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` for seamless content glide.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary bridges the strict classical geometry of the academy with the smooth tactile ergonomics demanded by mobile touch interfaces:

- **Radius Hierarchy (`roundedness: 2`):** 
  - Standard cards, interactive list containers, and modal sheets use 14px–16px (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`).
  - Buttons, segmented tabs, and form input fields use 10px–12px (`rounded-lg`).
  - Chips, status tags, and avatar placeholders use 6px–8px (`rounded-md`) or full pills where distinct separation is required.
- **Tactile Softness:** Avoids harsh zero-radius geometries, producing a refined, approachable mobile presence that feels comfortable under thumb navigation while remaining disciplined.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) with crisp white typography (`Work Sans`, Semi-bold, 14px), 48px height for thumb targets, and 12px border radius. Hover/active states shift to `#1A2B44`.
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined container with 1px border in `#0B192C` or `#E8E5E1`, pure white background, and deep navy text.
- **Accent Button:** Deep Burgundy (`#8B2635`) filled button reserved for vital administrative triggers (e.g., "Confirm Audition Result", "Publish Schedule").

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- 64px fixed height + device bottom safe area.
- Pure white translucent background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with backdrop blur).
- 4 to 5 icon-and-label tabs. Active tab renders in Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) or Ballet Gold (`#C5A059`) with a 3px top indicator line. Inactive tabs render in `#8C8C8C`.

### Cards & List Items
- **Student / Roster Card:** White background, 1px `#E8E5E1` border, 14px rounded corners, 16px internal padding. Left edge supports an optional 3px vertical status strip (Burgundy for alerts, Emerald for enrolled, Gold for honors).
- **Session List Tile:** Compact 56px row item with subtle hairline divider (`#EFECE8`), trailing chevron icon, and primary title in Oswald headline-sm.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Top-aligned labels in Oswald `label-caps` (`#5A5A5A`).
- Inputs: 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E8E5E1` border, 10px radius. On focus: 1.5px border in Deep Navy (`#0B192C`).
- Error state: 1.5px border in Alert Red (`#DC2626`) with red caption underneath.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** 24px height, 6px radius, uppercase Oswald 11px text with 8px horizontal padding. Styled with 10% tinted backgrounds (e.g., Emerald background `#EBF6F0` with `#1E7E4E` text for "Attending").
- **Filter Chips:** 36px height, rounded-full pill shape, background `#F4F3F1` with `#5A5A5A` text. Selected state flips to Deep Navy (`#0B192C`) background with white text.

### Top App Bar
- Compact 56px height. Warm ivory or white background with 1px bottom border.
- Centered or left-aligned Academy mark with Oswald screen title, right-aligned secondary actions (Notifications bell with unread burgundy dot, search trigger).
