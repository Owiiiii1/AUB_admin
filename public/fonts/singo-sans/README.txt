Singo is a valiant font family and extraordinary sans serif. Singo itself literally means Lion in Indonesia, which is the the strong and gallant animal just like this typeface. It has proportion of hard lines and smooth curves. Singo works great in any branding, logos, magazines, films, posters, websites, etc.
Singo features:
A full set of uppercase characters
Numbers & punctuation
Multilingual language support
PUA Encoded Characters
2360 Total glyphs
Singo includes:
Singo Regular (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Regular Italic (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Outline (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Outline Italic (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Shadow (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Shadow Italic (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Extruded (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Extruded Italic (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Rounded (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Rounded Italic (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Shadow (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
Singo Shadow Italic (OTF, TTF & WOFF)
